Feeding Guidelines

Feed according to the age, size, and activity of your cat. If fed alone, feed 1.0 oz for each pound of body weight daily. Your cat should have access to clean, fresh water. Refrigerate after opening.

Transitioning

Despite how much your feline friends ask for Soulistic's high-quality foods, it's best to transition them gradually onto a new food over a week. Start by mixing a small amount of Soulistic in with their old food and slowly increase the amount of Soulistic fed over 7 days. This will help your kitty adjust to their new Soulistic foods.
