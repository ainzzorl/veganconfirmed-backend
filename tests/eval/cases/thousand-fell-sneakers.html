Comfort System

The Thousand Fell Comfort System* guarantees your everyday sneaker keeps you comfortably on your feet.

1. LUXE VEGAN BIO–LEATHER

Only the highest quality vegan bio-leather for a quick break-in and real leather look.

2. CLASSIC LOOK

Elevate your everyday look with a perfectly classic silhouette. All comfort and style, zero waste.

3. SUPER SOFT ALOE VERA LINER

Sustainable tech with moisture wicking aloe vera inside liner.

4. ULTIMATE COMFORT CUSHIONED INSOLE

Recycled foam insole—molding and rebounding for enduring comfort.

Our Materials

Bio–Leather Upper

Made from rPET and corn waste resin from GMO-free European fields. Bio-based, PETA approved, and built to outperform traditional leather — with less reliance on petroleum and none of the waste.

Natural Rubber Outsole

Outsoles made from responsibly-sourced natural Amazonian rubber — no synthetic fillers. Durable, grippy, and built to last.

Bio Foam

Bio foam derived from soybean oil. Cushioned, comfortable, and built to move with you — without relying on petroleum-based materials.

Sugarcane

Insole board derived from sugarcane fibers from Brazilian food waste. Bio-based, flexible, and built to replace traditional plastic components.

Aloe Vera Lining

100% recycled polyester mesh lining, coated in microencapsulated aloe vera. Antimicrobial, cooling, and odor-resistant — no socks required.

Coconut Husk

Structural components made from coconut husk fibers responsibly-sourced in Brazil from food waste. Bio-based stability and support — no traditional plastic components.

I love these shoes. The color is very versatile, and the shoes are comfortable. The only negative thing I would say is the same thing I would say about other Thousand Fell shoes: their insoles are not the best. They do not stay in place. It's an easy fix with an aftermarket insole, though.
